# ADR 0018: Cooperative component budgets and direction retention

Date: 2026-10-09. Status: accepted for laboratory comparison only.

## Context

Different acquisition families and scalar-copy inventories cannot be priced by
proof counts alone. Per-method candidate caps omit preparation, carry verification
and retention, and do not create equivalent search domains.

## Decision

Add a separate equal-weight logical component model with raw phase vectors and
an explicitly zero-weight informational SHAKE-byte counter. Integrate sparse
bound1/2 and existing full-ball/MITM engines through owned streams/checkpoints.
Count preparation, source work, materialization, dense verification and retention.
Close/flush in finally; retain observed excess and independent stopping flags.

Commit completed proof-validation/retention transactions before the post-operation
stop. Exclude unchecked carry. Compare exact keys with primitive-direction keys,
retaining verified original vectors under both policies. Continue acquisition
after capacity to study this fixed policy rather than infer adaptive savings.

Freeze three profiles, three budgets, four methods, two retention policies and
four-step fresh/control cohorts. Independently check domain-filtered oracle sets,
paid common ledgers and native positive/negative verdicts. Bind model/source/
verifier/raw evidence and retain every empty/censored attempt.

## Consequences

All 576 steps and 12 complete-domain audits pass. More budget does not imply more
new proofs in every finite campaign; full-ball domains differ from sparse ones.
Direction keys reduce legal scalar-copy occupancy but establish neither
independent work nor a universal optimal policy. Maximum measured excess is 67
model units; no hard resource ceiling is claimed. Legacy exhaustion at a closed
yield may remain unobserved and is conservatively reported.

This model is arbitrary and excludes exhaustive allocator/bookkeeping pricing.
No hardware speed claim, production work rule, security parameter or consensus
change follows. An independent broader native baseline and more fresh schedules
precede portable work/security decisions. See the
[report](../research/cooperative-acquisition-v1.md).
